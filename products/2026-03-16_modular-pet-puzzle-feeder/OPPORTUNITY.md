### Candidate 1 — Modular Pet Puzzle Feeder with Swappable Difficulty Inserts (Wildcard — Carried 3 Cycles)

**Problem:** Dogs solve Level 1–2 puzzle feeders in minutes. Outward Hound (Nina Ottosson) sells 4 difficulty levels as separate products ($10–$25 each). Smart dog owners buy Level 1, their dog masters it in a day, then they buy Level 2, then Level 3. Each level is a separate $15–25 purchase. A modular base with swappable difficulty inserts lets one product grow with the dog — and creates recurring revenue from insert packs.

**Deep Findings:**
- **Outward Hound/Nina Ottosson pricing:** Level 1 $10–13, Level 2 $12–18, Level 3 $15–25, Level 4 $25–30. A dog owner buying all 4 levels spends $60–85.
- **Etsy 3D printed options:** PiperzLab sells a "Dog Maze Training Kit" STL with 6 difficulty levels (digital only, not physical). Has 39 favorites. Also an Interactive Dog Slow Feeder STL.
- **Cults3D:** juancv3d's "Interactive Dog Puzzle" features 1 base + 5 accessories, described as "super modular." This is the closest to our concept but is STL-only, not a physical product.
- **Physical Etsy sellers:** TheDogPuzzleShop sells laser-cut wood multi-level puzzles (~$25–40). LokiandPaws sells 3D printed puzzle toys from the UK.
- **Paws Dynasty 2026 trend article:** "Modular sets stack, rotate, and swap components like Lego for dogs." "Look for bowls with switch-out inserts so the same base can evolve." "Slide-in maze plates cost roughly a third of an entirely new toy."
- **Key gap:** No physical 3D printed modular puzzle feeder on Etsy with swappable inserts sold separately as expansion packs.

**Food Safety Approach:**
- PETG is considered food-safe as a raw material (no BPA)
- Layer lines harbor bacteria — mitigated by food-safe epoxy coating (Masterbond EP42HT-2FG or ArtResin)
- Alternative: use PETG with stainless steel nozzle + instruct buyers to hand-wash and coat with food-safe sealant
- For pet food contact (not human food), the bar is lower — PETG with proper cleaning is widely accepted in the pet product community
- Prusa recommends against direct food contact but pet food is brief contact, not long-term storage

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Outward Hound dominates with injection-molded plastic ($10–30 per level)
- No 3D printed physical modular system on Etsy
- STL-only options exist (PiperzLab, juancv3d) but no physical product
- Recurring revenue from insert packs is the key business model innovation
- Wood puzzle feeders exist ($25–40) but aren't modular/swappable
- Competition Gap: 5/5 for physical product, 3/5 for STL

### Scoring Rationale for Top Candidate

**Candidate 1 — Modular Pet Puzzle Feeder (4.2 avg) ✅ ADVANCING**
- **Pain (4):** Dog owners genuinely frustrated by having to buy 4 separate puzzles. Breed forums full of complaints about dogs solving puzzles too quickly. Smart breeds (Border Collies, Poodles, GSDs) need constant escalation.
- **Size (4):** 65+ million US households own dogs. Puzzle feeder market growing rapidly. Outward Hound is a major brand — validates the category.
- **Feasibility (3):** Most complex design in our portfolio. Multiple insert types, food-safe considerations, sliding/rotating mechanics. But individual pieces are small and printable. PETG + food-safe epoxy coating addresses safety.
- **Competition Gap (5):** No 3D printed physical modular puzzle feeder on Etsy. STL-only options exist but no ready-to-use physical product with swappable inserts sold as expansion packs. This is a blue ocean for the physical product.
- **Profit (5):** Base unit at $29.99–$39.99. Insert packs at $8.99–$12.99. A customer buying base + 3 insert packs = $57–$79. Recurring revenue model. Material cost per piece is low (~$1–3). Margin 70%+.

## Decision: Advancing Candidate 1 (Modular Pet Puzzle Feeder with Swappable Difficulty Inserts) as primary product.

**Rationale:** This product has been carried forward for 3 cycles (longest-held candidate in pipeline history) with consistent 4.0+ scores. The 5/5 competition gap and 5/5 profit potential make it the highest-ceiling product in our portfolio after the spice organizer. The recurring revenue model (insert packs) is a business model innovation unique in our lineup. While feasibility is lower (3/5) due to food-safety and design complexity, the payoff justifies the engineering investment.

**Product line strategy:** This launches the Pet Accessories wildcard niche as our third product line (Kitchen/Home primary, Desk/Keyboard secondary, Pet tertiary). Cat-specific inserts can extend the line in future cycles.
