## 3. Reddit Launch Post

**Target subreddit:** r/MechanicalKeyboards

**Title:** I 3D printed a TPU wrist rest sized exactly to my Q1 Pro — it's squishy, it bounces back, and it's exactly 332mm wide

**Body:**

I've tried foam wrist rests, gel wrist rests, and a wood one. The foam compressed after two months and developed a permanent dent. The gel was fine until summer when it got warm and sticky. The wood one looked beautiful but offered zero cushion — my wrists hurt after an hour.

So I printed one in TPU (Shore 95A, the flexible rubbery filament). The inside is a lattice structure — think of it like a grid of tiny flexible walls that compress when you press down and spring back when you lift up. It gives you that progressive cushion feel where it's soft on first contact but doesn't bottom out.

The key thing: I measured my Q1 Pro at 332mm wide and made the rest exactly that width. No overhang, no gap, just a clean match with the front edge of the keyboard. I made presets for all the common sizes (60%, 65%, 75%, TKL, full) based on Keychron, GMMK, and Ducky measurements.

TPU is honestly kind of annoying to print — you need a direct drive extruder, slow speeds (25mm/s), and careful retraction settings. But once it's dialed in, the material is perfect for this. It's grippy on the bottom so it doesn't slide, it's easy to wipe clean, and it doesn't compress permanently like foam.

Happy to answer questions about TPU printing or the lattice settings. I have a "soft" and "firm" version and the difference is dramatic.
