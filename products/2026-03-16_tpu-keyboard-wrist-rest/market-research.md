## 1. Reddit r/MechanicalKeyboards - User Pain Points & Complaints

### Sizing Mismatch (Top Complaint)
- Users consistently report wrist rests that are **too wide, too narrow, too long, or too short** for their specific keyboard
- One user on Grifiti rests: *"a bit too wide and short, but they are better than nothing"* - wished they were *"40% taller and smaller foot print"*
- A [H]ard|Forum user ordered Grifiti Fat 4" but switched to Slim: *"the Fat is just too fat"*
- Professional reviewer: Castle Bailey wooden rest was *"too wide"* and *"wasn't easy to use with a standard keyboard"*
- A rest that *"doesn't stretch the full length of a standard keyboard, making number pad use awkward"*
- Expert finding: Mismatches of **even 5mm** cause ulnar deviation or flexor tendon strain

### Material Complaints
- Leather coverings get **sweaty**
- Some foam rests are **too hard** or **too firm**
- Stock keyboard wrist rests (e.g., Razer Ornata): *"too hard, not enough foam and too high"*
- Mouse rests can be *"slightly overstuffed, making a comfortable position hard to find"*

### Height Mismatch
- Mechanical keyboards have **more height than standard keyboards**, making generic wrist rests problematic
- Low-profile keyboards (e.g., Logitech G915) need completely different rest heights
- A wrist rest that's too tall or too short *"can actually worsen posture, bending your wrists upward or downward"*

### Ergonomic Debate
- Some users argue wrist rests *"aren't good for your wrists"* - a recurring debate
- Counter-argument: with proper sizing, they reduce strain significantly
- One Reddit user with carpal tunnel reported **reduced strain within weeks** after getting a properly matched Keychron K6 magnetic palm rest

### Most Recommended Products (Reddit Consensus)
| Product | Key Feedback |
|---------|-------------|
| Glorious Wrist Rest | *"super thick, firm but gives enough to not hurt your wrist"* - best after 1 year of use |
| Grifiti Fat Wrist Pad | *"quality stuff"*, *"feels very good"* - but sizing complaints common |
| HyperX Wrist Rest | Most popular on Amazon, cooling gel + memory foam |
| NPKC Wooden | Premium black walnut/cherry, popular aesthetically |

**KEY INSIGHT:** The #1 gap is **keyboard-specific sizing**. Users are buying generic "TKL" or "60%" size categories and finding they don't actually match their specific board dimensions.

### KEY FINDING: There is virtually NO commercial seller of TPU lattice wrist rests sized to specific keyboard models.

The designs exist as free files but:
1. Most people don't own 3D printers (especially ones that handle TPU well)
2. Free designs are generic, not keyboard-model-specific
3. Nobody has created a product line covering multiple popular keyboards
4. The HIVE rest is the closest competitor but uses rigid PLA body with TPU inserts (different approach)

### TPU Printing Considerations
- Shore hardness range: **85A (softest) to 95A (firmest)**
- 85A gives maximum cushion, 95A gives maximum support
- Lattice/gyroid infill patterns control firmness and breathability
- Multi-material (TPU body + PLA base) is the optimal approach
- Print time: ~8 hours per rest
- Material cost: ~60g TPU ($2-4) + PLA for base ($0.50-1)

- Keychron Q1: front height **22.6mm** (without keycaps)

- Feet height: **2.4mm** (adds when feet deployed)

**This is the key differentiation: a wrist rest that matches not just WIDTH but also the front-edge HEIGHT of each specific keyboard model.**

### Recommended Launch Strategy
1. **Start with 75% layout** (Keychron Q1/V1 + GMMK Pro) - fastest growing segment, highest enthusiasm
2. **Add 65% layout** (Keychron Q2/V2) - second priority
3. **Add TKL** (Keychron Q3, GMMK 3) - third priority
4. **Offer 3 firmness options** per size: Soft (85A), Medium (90A), Firm (95A)
5. **Color matching** to popular keycap colorways (GMK sets, etc.)
6. **Product naming:** "Precision-Fit TPU Lattice Wrist Rest for [Keyboard Model]"

### Key Differentiators vs. Existing Market
1. **Keyboard-model-specific fit** (matched to exact width AND front-edge height)
2. **TPU lattice** = breathable + cushioning (vs. rigid wood/resin/PLA)
3. **Tunable firmness** via lattice pattern (honeycomb/stripes/diamond)
4. **Washable** (TPU can be rinsed, unlike foam/leather)
5. **Won't get sweaty** (open lattice airflow vs. solid foam/leather)
