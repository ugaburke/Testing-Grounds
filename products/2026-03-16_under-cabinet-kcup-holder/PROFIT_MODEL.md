## Profit Model Selection: Hybrid (Physical-Primary)

### Why Physical-Primary Hybrid?

The under-cabinet K-Cup holder follows the same pattern as the spice drawer organizer: the target buyer is a **non-printer-owning kitchen organizer** who wants a ready-made solution. K-Cup owners (40% of US households) overwhelmingly buy convenience products — they chose pods over manual coffee for a reason.

**Physical is primary** because:
- Target customer is a convenience-first buyer (chose K-Cups over pour-over/French press)
- Under-cabinet mounting requires precise fit — buyers want confidence it works out of the box
- Small per-unit print (~30 minutes, <$1 material) means high margin on physical sales
- Repeat purchase behavior confirmed by Etsy Star Seller reviews ("needed just a couple more to expand my storage")

**STL is secondary** because:
- The 3D printing community will want to print their own
- Parametric source allows customization for unusual cabinet thicknesses
- Pod-type presets (K-Cup, Nespresso Vertuo, Nespresso Original) add value over free generic STLs
- Lower price point than spice organizer STL — simpler design

| Free STLs (MakerWorld) | Free | $0 | Single-size, countertop designs |

### Our Positioning

- **vs. Etsy Star Seller ($18–22):** Our Single at $14.99 is cheaper. Our pod-type presets (K-Cup + Nespresso) are a differentiator they don't offer.
- **vs. Carousel towers ($35–45):** We're under-cabinet (saves counter space) at a lower price. Different value proposition.
- **vs. Amazon basics ($12–20):** Price-competitive but we offer under-cabinet mounting + pod-specific sizing. Amazon holders are countertop.
- **vs. Free STLs ($0):** Our STL at $4.99 adds pod-type presets, parametric cabinet thickness, and print guide. Free designs are single-size.
