### Candidate 1 — Under-Cabinet K-Cup/Coffee Pod Holder (Parametric, Multi-Pod)

**Problem:** Kitchen counter space is precious. K-Cup holders sit on countertops, taking up valuable real estate. An under-cabinet mounted holder hides pods out of sight while keeping them accessible. One Etsy seller (listing 933843890) has Star Seller status with 4.8+ reviews — buyer says "I love this k-cup storage solution. I have had some for a few years, but decided I needed just a couple more to expand my storage."

**Deep Findings:**
- Etsy has multiple active K-Cup holder sellers: under-cabinet hooks, carousel towers ($38), drawer inserts
- Star Seller at listing 933843890 with repeat buyers ("needed just a couple more to expand my storage")
- Free STL alternatives exist on MakerWorld and Cults3D, but most are single-size or countertop designs
- Under-cabinet mount = mechanical (hook or screw), not adhesive — aligns with our "adhesive fails" insight
- K-Cup/Nespresso Vertuo/Nespresso Original pods have DIFFERENT dimensions — opportunity for pod-specific presets (same pattern as spice organizer brand presets)
- K-Cup: 48mm top dia x 35mm bottom dia x 42mm tall
- Nespresso Original: 37mm dia x 26mm tall
- Nespresso Vertuo: 56mm top dia x 54mm tall (standard), or 27mm tall (espresso)

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Etsy sellers offer K-Cup specific or Nespresso specific, rarely both
- No parametric design with pod-type presets
- Under-cabinet hook designs don't work on all cabinet thicknesses — a clamp version could be universal
- Free designs on MakerWorld/Cults3D compete with STL sales

### Candidate 2 — Stackable Fridge Can Dispenser (Gravity-Feed, PETG)

**Problem:** Canned drinks (soda, sparkling water, beer) roll around in the fridge. Gravity-feed can dispensers organize them into neat rows. MostlyPrinted on Etsy is a Star Seller with 5-star reviews on their mini can dispenser — "Perfectly printed and sized; cans roll nicely."

**Sources:**
- Etsy: MostlyPrinted Star Seller, 7.5oz mini can dispenser, stackable
- Etsy listing 4340619950: Stackable fridge organizer in PETG, wave pattern
- Amazon: 3D Printed 8-can dispenser, PETG, $15–25
- Printables: Multiple free can dispenser designs (high competition)

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- MostlyPrinted specializes in mini cans (7.5oz) — standard 12oz cans are underserved
- Free designs on Printables/Thingiverse are numerous
- Differentiation opportunity: parametric sizing for different can sizes (7.5oz, 12oz, 16oz tall boys, slim cans)
- PETG for fridge temp resistance is a selling point
- Moderate competition — multiple Etsy sellers and free alternatives

### Candidate 8 — Under-Sink Pipe-Clearance Organizer (Parametric)

**Problem:** Under-sink storage is universally terrible because the pipes are in the way. Every cabinet has different pipe configurations. Amazon organizers ($15–30) use expandable shelves that still don't fit around pipes properly. A parametric design where the buyer enters pipe positions + cabinet dimensions = custom fit.

**Sources:**
- Amazon "under sink organizer" — common sizing complaints
- No 3D printed under-sink solutions found on Etsy
- Parametric pipe-clearance cutouts would be genuinely novel
- Same "custom-fit" pattern as spice organizer

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Amazon expandable shelves don't properly accommodate pipe configurations
- No 3D printed solutions exist — true blue ocean
- Design challenge: pipes vary wildly, need multiple parametric cutout positions
- Print size concern: under-sink organizers may be too large for typical print beds
- Feasibility: 3/5 due to size and complexity constraints

### Scoring Rationale for Top Candidates

**Candidate 1 — Under-Cabinet K-Cup Holder (4.2 avg) ✅ ADVANCING**
- **Pain (3):** Counter clutter is real but not urgent. "Nice to have" more than "need to have." Repeat buyer behavior confirms satisfaction.
- **Size (5):** 40% of US households own a single-serve coffee maker. K-Cups are the dominant pod format. Massive addressable market.
- **Feasibility (5):** Simple hook/clamp geometry. Small print (~30min per module). No moving parts beyond the hook. PETG for kitchen use.
- **Competition Gap (4):** Under-cabinet is less common than countertop. Pod-type presets (K-Cup vs. Nespresso Vertuo vs. Nespresso Original) would be novel. Existing sellers are pod-format-specific, not parametric.
- **Profit (4):** Physical at $15–22 per holder. STL at $4–5. Material cost <$1 per holder. Repeat purchase pattern confirmed by buyer reviews. Pod variety enables product line expansion.

**Candidate 6 — Pet Puzzle Feeder (4.0 avg) — backup, held for Cycle 5**
**Candidate 8 — Under-Sink Organizer (4.0 avg) — backup, feasibility concern (size)**

## Decision: Advancing Candidate 1 (Under-Cabinet K-Cup/Pod Holder) as primary product.

**Rationale:** Continues our Kitchen/Home niche focus with a proven product pattern (under-cabinet mount, Star Seller validation). The pod-type preset system (K-Cup, Nespresso Vertuo, Nespresso Original) mirrors our successful brand-specific approach from the spice organizer. Massive addressable market (40% of US households). Clean design challenge — simple, small, printable, repeatable.

**Product line strategy:** This product launches the "kitchen under-cabinet" sub-line alongside the spice drawer organizer, building toward a coherent "3D printed kitchen organization" brand on Etsy.
