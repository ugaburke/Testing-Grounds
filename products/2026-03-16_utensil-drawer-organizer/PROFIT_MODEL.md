## Profit Model Selection: Hybrid (Physical-Primary)

### Why Physical-Primary Hybrid?

Same pattern as the kitchen line. The target buyer is a homeowner frustrated with cheap expandable organizers, unwilling to spend $129–199 on wood, and doesn't own a 3D printer. The physical product at $65–110 hits the sweet spot.

**Physical is primary** because:
- Custom-fit is the core value — buyer provides drawer dimensions, we deliver a perfect-fit organizer
- Contoured utensil profiles are the differentiator — this requires design expertise the buyer can't replicate
- Wood custom organizers at $129–199 create massive pricing headroom
- AOV potential: multi-drawer orders, "whole kitchen" bundles

**STL is a strong secondary** because:
- Etsy's new Drawer Organizer Generator listing proves demand for parametric source files
- Maker community actively searches for drawer organizer STLs
- Our contoured-slot presets differentiate from every free rectangular-grid STL
