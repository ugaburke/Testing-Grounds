## Profit Model Selection: Hybrid (Physical-Primary)

### Why Physical-Primary Hybrid?

The FIFO can dispenser follows the established Kitchen/Home pattern: target buyer is a **home organizer who wants a ready-made solution** that fits their specific pantry. However, there's a significant STL opportunity given that one STL seller alone has 10,600+ sales — the DIY market is proven and large.

**Physical is primary** because:
- Target customer is a pantry-organizing home cook, not a maker
- Parametric shelf depth needs to be right — buyers want it to fit their specific pantry
- Repeat purchase behavior is exceptionally strong (buyers ordering 5+ units)
- Higher AOV per customer than smaller kitchen products

**STL is a strong secondary** because:
- 10,600+ sales from one STL seller proves massive DIY demand
- The rebeltaz free design is popular but uses 1kg filament — our optimized design adds value
- Parametric source with shelf depth + can size presets differentiates from free fixed-size STLs

### Why This Pricing Works

**Single Lane at $19.99:**
- Cheaper than Amazon OnDisplay FIFO ($15–25) when you consider our parametric fit
- Competitive with physical Etsy sellers ($25–35) for a single lane
- The multi-lane packs ($34.99–$47.99) offer volume discounts that Amazon products can't match
- Parametric shelf depth = guaranteed fit, which no competitor offers

**Full STL at $7.99:**
- Premium over ElainesSweetLife ($3–5) justified by parametric source + 4 can presets + 4 shelf depths
- Premium over free rebeltaz design justified by optimized filament usage (200g vs. 1kg) + modular lanes
- The filament optimization alone saves the buyer ~$16 in material cost per unit
