# PROFIT_MODEL.md — Under-Desk Headphone Mount

## Product: Under-Desk Headphone Mount with Integrated Cable Channel

## Profit Model Recommendation: Hybrid (Digital-First)

### Rationale

The under-desk headphone mount is an ideal **hybrid** product with a **digital-first** launch strategy:

1. **Digital (STL) is the primary channel.** The target buyer — a desk-setup enthusiast, gamer, or WFH worker — disproportionately owns a 3D printer or knows someone who does. The r/functionalprint and r/battlestations communities overlap heavily with printer ownership. STL sales at $3.99–$4.99 on Etsy and Printables Store have zero fulfillment cost and infinite scale. The parametric nature (adjustable desk thickness, headband width) adds value that justifies paying for a file over grabbing a free generic clip.

2. **Physical sales capture the non-printer market.** Many people searching "headphone holder" on Etsy don't own printers. A printed-and-shipped option at $14.99–$19.99 (PETG, choice of colors) captures this segment. Print cost is ~$0.50 filament + 1.5 hours machine time. At $16.99 average sale price minus ~$5 shipping/packaging and ~$2.50 Etsy fees, margin is ~$9/unit before machine amortization.

3. **Digital-first de-risks the launch.** No inventory, no shipping logistics. If the STL sells, we validate demand before investing in physical fulfillment. If physical demand appears (buyers asking "can you print this for me?"), we add the physical listing.

### Why Not Physical-Only?

Physical-only would leave money on the table from the maker community, which is the most likely first-adopter segment. These buyers want the STL so they can customize color, infill, and size. Forcing them to buy a printed product when they own a printer creates friction, not revenue.

### Why Not Digital-Only?

Etsy search traffic for "headphone holder" and "headphone stand" skews toward buyers who want a finished product. Ignoring this segment means competing only in the smaller STL-buyer pool. The hybrid model captures both.

### Pricing Justification

- **STL at $4.49:** Comparable to Etsy STL listings for functional desk accessories ($3–7 range). The parametric customization and engineered stress-relief design justify mid-range pricing.
- **Physical at $16.99:** Amazon adhesive headphone mounts range $8–15. Our clamp design is more durable and customizable. $16.99 positions above cheap Amazon imports while undercutting premium desk accessories ($25+).
