## 3. Reddit Launch Post

**Target subreddit:** r/functionalprint

**Title:** I designed an under-desk headphone mount after my fifth one cracked at the clamp

**Body:**

I've been through probably five different headphone mount designs from Thingiverse and they all eventually crack at the same spot — right where the arm meets the clamp body. Sharp inside corner + layer lines perpendicular to the stress = guaranteed failure.

So I sat down and designed one from scratch in OpenSCAD with the specific goal of not cracking:

- 8mm fillets at every inside corner to spread the load
- Captive M6 nut instead of printed threads (those always strip)
- Sized for real headphones — 60mm hook width fits my HD6XX and DT 770 with room to spare
- Cable channel through the hook arm so the cord isn't dangling

Two pieces, prints in about 2.5 hours, no supports. I've been using it daily for three weeks and it's solid.

Printed in PETG on my P1S. The parametric OpenSCAD source is adjustable for different desk thicknesses (15–40mm) and headband widths.

Happy to share more details on the design decisions if anyone's interested. This is honestly the most "why didn't I do this sooner" print I've done.
