## Profit Model Selection: Hybrid (Physical-Primary)

### Why Physical-Primary Hybrid?

Same pattern as the K-Cup holder and spice organizer: the target buyer is a **home cook who wants a ready-made solution**, not a 3D printing enthusiast. The buyer already has a tablet/phone and is looking for a kitchen-specific mount — they want it to work out of the box with their specific device.

**Physical is primary** because:
- Target customer is a home cook, not a maker — they'll pay for convenience
- Device-specific sizing needs to be right — buyers want confidence it fits their iPad/iPhone
- Print time is moderate (~1.5–2 hours) but material cost is still low
- The "under-cabinet" product line now has 3 products enabling bundle upsells

**STL is secondary** because:
- Maker community will want to customize for their specific device and cabinet
- Parametric source with device presets adds value over free single-device STLs
- Lower friction for the 3D printing audience than buying physical

## Competitive Pricing Analysis

| Competitor | Platform | Price | Mount | Device Fit |
|-----------|----------|-------|-------|-----------|
| Star Seller (listing 717840839) | Etsy | ~$15–20 | Hook, under-cabinet | Phone only |
| PETG holder (listing 1844707766) | Etsy | ~$18–22 | Hook, no-tools | Phone + small tablets |
| iPad recipe stand (listing 4341476173) | Etsy | ~$20–25 | Hook, tool-free | iPad only, 2 cabinet types |
| Belkin kitchen mount | Amazon | $30–40 | Adhesive/screw | iPad specific |
| Free STLs (Cults3D/Thingiverse) | Free | $0 | Various | Single-device |

### Our Positioning

- **vs. Etsy Star Seller phone mount ($15–20):** We offer both phone AND tablet sizes with device presets. Same price tier but more versatile.
- **vs. iPad recipe stand ($20–25):** We support all device sizes parametrically, not just iPad. Same hook mount but adjustable cabinet thickness.
- **vs. Belkin ($30–40):** Half the price, no adhesive (which fails in kitchens), and device-size specific.
- **vs. Free STLs ($0):** Our parametric source with device presets + angle adjustment adds genuine value over single-device free designs.
