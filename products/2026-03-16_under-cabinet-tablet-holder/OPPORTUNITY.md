### Candidate 1 — Under-Cabinet Tablet/Phone Holder for Recipes (Hook Clamp Mount)

**Problem:** Cooks constantly prop tablets/phones against canisters or lean them on the backsplash while following recipes. Screens get splashed, counters get crowded, and angles are bad. An under-cabinet hook-clamp holder (same mount pattern as our K-Cup holder) keeps the device at eye level, protected from splashes, and off the counter.

**Deep Findings:**
- Etsy listing 717840839: Star Seller under-cabinet phone mount for recipes, 5-star reviews. Reviewer concern: "phone doesn't fit under the closed cabinet as shown"
- Etsy listing 1844707766: PETG phone & tablet holder, no-tools hook mount, supports iPhones and small tablets
- Etsy listing 4341476173: iPad recipe stand, tool-free cabinet mount, fits two common cabinet types
- Cults3D: Kitchen cabinet tablet holder STL, no screws/adhesive
- Thingiverse thing:5365764: Fold-away tablet holder, 9 printed parts, screw mount
- Free STLs exist but most are either phone-only OR tablet-only, not adjustable

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Star Sellers exist but are device-size-specific (phone mount vs. tablet mount)
- No parametric design that adjusts slot width for phone vs. tablet vs. iPad
- Our hook-clamp mount pattern from the K-Cup holder can be reused — consistent "under-cabinet" brand
- Device-width presets (iPhone, iPad Mini, iPad, Samsung Galaxy Tab) would mirror our pod-type/brand-specific pattern
- Angle-adjustable shelf for viewing while cooking would differentiate
- PETG for kitchen steam/splash resistance

### Candidate 2 — Kitchen Wrap/Foil Dispenser (Under-Cabinet or Cabinet Door)

**Problem:** Aluminum foil, cling wrap, and parchment paper boxes clutter drawers and are annoying to use. A wall-mount or under-cabinet dispenser keeps rolls accessible with a built-in cutter.

**Sources:**
- Etsy listing 1839343068: 3D printable modular foil organizer STL, fits rolls up to 62mm dia
- MakerWorld: Free foil/cling wrap holder (simple, adhesive mount)
- Amazon: Wall-mounted wrap dispensers $15–30, bamboo options popular
- Multiple free STL designs on Thingiverse and MakerWorld

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Well-served on Amazon (bamboo, plastic, metal options $15–30)
- Free 3D printable designs exist (MakerWorld, Thingiverse)
- The cutter mechanism is the hard part — hard to 3D print a sharp, safe cutter
- Roll diameter varies significantly between brands
- Moderate competition, moderate differentiation opportunity

### Candidate 3 — Utensil Drawer Organizer (Parametric Custom Fit)

**Problem:** Kitchen utensil drawers are universally messy. Generic organizers don't fit non-standard drawer sizes.

**Sources:**
- MakerWorld: "Ultimate Gridfinity Kitchen Drawer Organizer" — free, popular
- MakerWorld: "Ultimate Parametric Drawer Organizer System" — free, fully customizable
- Printables: Parametric Utensil Drawer Organizer (OpenSCAD) — free
- MakerWorld: Customizable Drawer Organizer — free
- GitHub: wbu/drawer_organizer — fully parametric modular system

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- **Heavily saturated with FREE parametric designs** — Gridfinity system dominates
- Multiple free OpenSCAD parametric designs on Printables and MakerWorld
- Very difficult to charge for an STL when superior free options exist
- Physical sales possible but Gridfinity compatibility expected
- Competition Gap: 1/5 — this space is owned by free designs

### Candidate 5 — Measuring Cup & Spoon Holder (Under-Cabinet Hook Mount)

**Problem:** Measuring cups and spoons get lost in drawers or tangled on hooks. A dedicated under-cabinet mount keeps them visible and accessible.

**Sources:**

- Printables: Magnetic measuring cup and spoon holder (free STL)

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**

- Amazon metal/plastic options are cheap ($10–15)
- Our hook-clamp mount would add value (no screws/adhesive)
- Brand-specific presets less applicable here (measuring sets are more standardized)
- Moderate opportunity — decent product but limited differentiation

### Candidate 6 — Modular Pet Puzzle Feeder with Difficulty Inserts (Wildcard — Carried Forward)

**Problem:** Carried forward from Cycle 2/3/4. Dogs solve Level 1–2 puzzles in minutes. Outward Hound sells difficulty levels as separate $10–25 SKUs. A modular system with swappable inserts creates recurring revenue.

**Sources:**
- Cycle 2 background agent: detailed research with forum evidence
- BuzzFeed, Whole Dog Journal, breed forums
- No existing modular system with swappable inserts on Etsy
- Outward Hound pricing: $10–25 per difficulty level

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Genuinely novel product concept — no modular swappable system exists
- Recurring revenue from insert packs ($8–12 each)
- Food-safety coating requirement adds production complexity (food-safe epoxy or PETG)
- Higher design/engineering effort than typical products
- Competition Gap: 5/5 — truly uncontested

### Candidate 7 — Pantry Can Organizer / Shelf Riser (Stackable)

**Problem:** Canned goods in the pantry end up stacked randomly. You can't see what's behind the front row. Tiered shelf risers solve this but never fit the exact shelf dimensions.

**Sources:**
- Amazon: Can organizers $15–25, mostly plastic/metal
- Printables: Some free can dispenser designs
- MakerWorld: Limited pantry-specific organizers
- Etsy: Few 3D printed pantry organizers

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Amazon dominates with cheap plastic/wire options ($10–20)
- Free 3D printed designs are sparse for pantry-specific use
- Size matters: pantry shelves vary widely, parametric sizing has value
- Large print size may exceed typical build plates (220mm+)
- Moderate opportunity — size constraint limits feasibility

---

### Candidate 8 — Under-Cabinet LED Light Diffuser / Cover (Parametric)

**Problem:** LED strip lights under cabinets look harsh and cheap without a diffuser. Commercial diffuser channels are cut-to-length aluminum. A 3D printed snap-on cover could be cheaper and customizable.

**Sources:**
- Amazon: LED channel/diffuser kits $12–25
- Limited 3D printed options found
- Niche crossover with home improvement

**Current Solutions & Gaps:**
- Aluminum channels are cheap, well-established on Amazon
- 3D printed plastic may not diffuse light as well as frosted polycarbonate
- Very niche — most people buy aluminum channels
- Low demand signal from Etsy/maker community
- Competition Gap: 2/5 — hard to beat aluminum on price or quality

### Scoring Rationale for Top Candidates

**Candidate 1 — Under-Cabinet Tablet Holder (4.0 avg) ✅ ADVANCING**
- **Pain (4):** Real daily friction for home cooks. Propping devices is universally annoying and risks splash damage. Higher pain than K-Cup storage.
- **Size (5):** Massive market — nearly every household has a phone/tablet, and cooking from digital recipes is mainstream.
- **Feasibility (4):** Moderate complexity — needs angle-adjustable shelf + hook clamp. Larger than K-Cup holder but well within print capabilities. Multiple parts may be needed.
- **Competition Gap (3):** Star Sellers exist, but no parametric design adjusting for device width. Our hook-clamp mount + device-width presets adds differentiation. Not a blue ocean but has a clear angle.
- **Profit (4):** Physical at $18–25. STL at $4.99. Same margin profile as K-Cup holder. Strengthens the "under-cabinet" product line.

**Candidate 6 — Pet Puzzle Feeder (4.0 avg) — backup, carried to Cycle 6**
- Genuinely novel but high design complexity. Food-safety requirement adds production friction. Saving for a dedicated wildcard cycle.

## Decision: Advancing Candidate 1 (Under-Cabinet Tablet/Phone Holder for Recipes) as primary product.

**Rationale:** Extends the "kitchen under-cabinet" product sub-line (now 3 products: spice organizer, K-Cup holder, tablet holder). Reuses the hook-clamp mount pattern. Device-width presets (iPhone, iPad Mini, iPad, Galaxy Tab) continue our brand-specific sizing strategy. High pain point (4/5) — cooks genuinely struggle with device placement. Massive addressable market.

**Product line strategy:** This completes a trilogy of under-cabinet kitchen products that can be cross-sold as a bundle. "The Ultimate Kitchen Under-Cabinet Kit: spice organizer + pod holder + recipe display."
