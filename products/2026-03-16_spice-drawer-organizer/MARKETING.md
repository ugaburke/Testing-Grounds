**Printed in PETG** — heat-resistant, moisture-resistant, and won't warp like PLA near your stove. Dishwasher-safe top rack.

**Starter Kit** fits a standard 15" x 18" drawer with ~60 jar slots. Need a different size? Enter your exact dimensions and we'll calculate the perfect layout.

## 3. Reddit Launch Post

**Target subreddit:** r/functionalprint

**Title:** I measured every major spice brand's jars and made a modular drawer organizer with brand-specific slots

**Body:**

My wife bought a bamboo spice organizer from Amazon. Half the jars wobbled, the Trader Joe's rectangular jars didn't fit at all, and the whole thing was 2mm too wide for our drawer. Classic.

So I measured every jar in our spice drawer with calipers. Then I measured jars from 5 other brands at the grocery store. Turns out there's a surprising amount of variation:

- McCormick: 46mm round
- Trader Joe's: 46mm rectangular (not round!)
- Simply Organic: 52mm square (French square glass)
- Whole Foods 365: 50mm round
- Penzeys: 52mm round

I built a parametric OpenSCAD module that holds 2 jars each, with dovetail snap joints on all 4 sides. You pick your brand preset, enter your drawer dimensions, and it tells you how many modules to print. The modules snap together in both X and Y — no glue, no screws.

Printed in PETG because the drawer is next to the stove. Each module is about 18g and takes 30 minutes. Our full drawer (30 modules) took a weekend of batch printing.

The cool part: we have a mix of McCormick and Trader Joe's, so I printed some modules with round slots and some with rectangular. They all snap together because the exterior dimensions are identical regardless of which brand preset you use.

Been using it for a month. Zero jars wobbling, zero rattling when the drawer opens. My wife is pleased, which is the real success metric.
